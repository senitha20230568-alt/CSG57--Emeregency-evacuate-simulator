How EVACUATE Works

Discover how EVACUATE transforms a 2D building floor plan into an interactive 3D emergency evacuation simulation.

Step 1: Upload a Floor Plan

Users upload a 2D floor plan of the building they want to simulate. The floor plan acts as the starting point for creating the virtual environment.

Step 2: AI Floor-Plan Recognition

The AI analyses the uploaded floor plan and identifies important building elements such as walls, rooms, doors and windows.

Step 3: Review and Correct the Building Layout

Users can review the recognised building layout and make corrections if any elements have been incorrectly identified.

Step 4: Generate the 3D Building

The processed floor-plan information is used to generate a 3D representation of the building inside Unreal Engine.

Step 5: Configure an Emergency Scenario

Users configure an emergency scenario by selecting the emergency type, hazard location, difficulty and other simulation settings.

Step 6: Run the Evacuation Simulation

Users enter the virtual environment and experience the emergency scenario. The simulation includes predefined character movements, environmental hazards and evacuation routes.

Step 7: View Results and Performance

After the simulation, users can review the results, including evacuation time, the number of occupants evacuated and other performance measurements.
