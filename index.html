What is EVACSIM?

EVACSIM is a proposed training and evaluation tool that helps users explore building layouts, configure emergency scenarios and assess how occupants can evacuate safely.

Learn More

What Can You Do With EVACSIM?

Explore the main stages of preparing and evaluating an emergency evacuation, from recognising a floor plan to reviewing simulation results.

AI Floor-Plan Recognition

Upload a 2D building floor plan and use AI to identify key elements such as rooms, walls, doors and windows.

3D Building Generation

Use the recognised floor-plan information to create an interactive 3D representation of the building.

Emergency Simulation

Configure emergency situations, such as fire scenarios, and practise evacuation procedures in the generated building.

Simulation Results

Review evacuation time and other performance measures to understand how effectively the scenario was handled.
